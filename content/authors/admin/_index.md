## About Me

Professor Sofiane KHELLADI is a leading researcher in fluid dynamics and energy systems. His work encompasses advanced numerical methods, multi-phase and complex flows, multi-physics coupling, and the aero-hydrodynamics and acoustics of turbomachinery. With a solid foundation in computational fluid dynamics (CFD) and computational aeroacoustics (CAA), his research emphasizes theoretical and numerical approaches, consistently supported by experimental validation to ensure the accuracy and practical applicability of his models. He integrates fluid mechanics, thermodynamics, and acoustics to optimize the performance of fluid and energy systems. Additionally, he has made significant contributions to the rheology of flows in manufacturing processes, with a strong focus on the development and deployment of numerical methods for diverse applications.

Over the past 18 years, Prof. Khelladi has supervised more than 40 PhD students and post-doctoral researchers. He has authored over 100 articles in JCR journals, several book chapters, and holds multiple patents. Since 2020, he has been the Director of the Fluid Engineering and Energy Systems Laboratory (LIFSE) and, since 2018, the Director of the ISYTHERM joint laboratory for thermal management in automotive mobility in collaboration with Valeo Thermal Systems. As the Principal Investigator of numerous academic and industrial projects, he has developed several computational codes, some of which are actively used in the aerospace and automotive industries.
